Profile & Mission

Bridging Clinical Precision & Data Science

Core Professional Focus

Graduated from Hacettepe University Faculty of Medicine (English) in 2005 and completed pathology residency in 2010. Conducted advanced research on pancreatobiliary neoplasms at Emory University School of Medicine (Atlanta, USA) under Prof. Volkan Adsay. Contributions have helped refine international tumor classification and staging guidelines.

Currently serving as Associate Professor of Pathology at Memorial Healthcare Group in Istanbul, directing digital primary diagnosis workflows and advancing routine WSI adoption across clinical laboratories.
