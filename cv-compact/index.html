Professional Summary

Board-certified surgical pathologist and Associate Professor with 20 years of clinical and academic experience. Subspecialty expertise in Pancreatobiliary & Gastrointestinal Pathology, with seminal contributions to international staging and WHO tumor classification systems through postdoctoral research at Emory University School of Medicine. Leader in Digital Pathology routine primary diagnostics (WSI) and developer of open-source clinical analytics tools (ClinicoPath Jamovi module, Patoloji Atlası).

Core Competencies

Clinical Pathology: Pancreatobiliary Pathology, GI Biopsies, Frozen Section, AJCC/UICC Staging, Immunohistochemistry.
Digital Pathology & AI: Routine WSI Primary Diagnosis, Telepathology, Quality Assurance, QuPath, Aiforia Integration.
Informatics & Software: R / Tidyverse, Jamovi Module Development (jmvtools), Survival Analysis, webR (Wasm), Git.
